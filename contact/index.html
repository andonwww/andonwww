F.A.Q.

The questions frequently asked by our clients, vendors, and peers are updated periodically. For any other queries or comments, please use the Message Us form or the Social Connections links on this page. For stock media requests use our AND/3000ad store link below.

Store Message
Do I pay for an estimate?

All estimates and quotes are free of charge. If you send us a detailed request, our quote is also binding and we won’t change it unless you have major revisions to the initial scope of services.

Do you sell royalty free media on this website?

The quick answer is yes, we sell every RF (royalty-free) asset at half the price (50%) listed on our microstock websites. For this, you need to contact us specifying the link to the asset you want to purchase. To speed up the process, please note the website where you found it and the image/video ID of that site’s collection. Once again, we will offer you HALF the price, if you contact us via this website.

Do you customize microstock media?

Yes. We developed and own all the 3D models rendered and animated in our collections on every microstock website displaying one of our galleries. We can custom design and re-design every one of them per client specifications, and then deliver the finals in any illustration or video format.

Do you deliver the website with the images?

Yes, once you approve your web design final look, we will purchase the images/videos for you on a RF (royalty-free) license using our discounted contributor accounts, passing all the savings to you. Unless otherwise specified, all media is included in the initial price quote.
